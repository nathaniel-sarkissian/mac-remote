3D flowers and triangular grass grow from an invisible, simplex-noise hillside. Each blade and petal is projected into 2D and hatched.

01 THE MEADOW

Simplex noise tilts neighboring flower stems together. Zero disables segment noise; stems still sprout along the local ground normal.

02 TERRAIN SURFACE

Percent grade. Ground rises away from the camera.

Smaller = finer terrain undulations.

03 TRIANGULAR GRASS

Base count for flat ground. Steeper projected terrain automatically generates more blades; actual count appears below the artwork.

Grass follows terrain left/right in the camera-facing plane. Vertical hatch marks follow each blade.

05 HATCHING

Jitter is applied after the original hatch segments are clipped. Grass hatch direction follows its projected upward axis rather than the flower hatch angle.
